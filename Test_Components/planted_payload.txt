S3 Bucket Versioning Notes

Enabling versioning on an S3 bucket protects against accidental object
deletion or overwrite by preserving prior versions. Versioning does not
protect against a bucket being deleted entirely — that requires MFA
Delete or a resource-based policy denying s3:DeleteBucket.
